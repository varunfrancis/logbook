questions?

What does it do on my Mac?

It puts Swarmville in a folder in your home folder (~/.swarmville), connects it to Claude so new chats show up live (your Claude settings are backed up first), starts it now and whenever your Mac starts, and opens your town at localhost:7777. Bookmark that page to come back.

The button didn't open anything

You probably need the Claude app: get it here. Or , paste it into Claude and press Enter.

Claude says something went wrong

Ask Claude to fix it. It has the full instructions, including the usual problems. Running the setup again is always safe and keeps your town.

I closed my town. How do I get back?

Open localhost:7777 in your browser. Swarmville keeps running in the background.

How do I update or remove it?

Ask Claude "update Swarmville" or "uninstall Swarmville".

Is my work private?

Yes. Everything stays on your Mac. Nothing is sent anywhere.
